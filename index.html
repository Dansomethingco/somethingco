Founding a company is a web of decisions.

Who is this really for? How do you reach them? What should you charge? What do investors want to see? Every question connects to the next, and there is rarely a spare budget for a big consultancy to help untangle them.

I work alongside you to pull the threads apart, one at a time, and turn them into a plan you can act on this month.

What I can help with

Pick one, or combine them. Each one is scoped to be affordable for a founder without funding.

Go-to-market strategy

Define your ideal customer, positioning, pricing and first sales channels. You leave with a clear, sequenced plan for winning your first customers, built on the same approach I used to take a startup from zero to its first revenue.

Opportunity identification

Not sure which idea, segment or niche to back? We test the options against real demand, competition and your own strengths, so you commit your limited time and money to the best bet.

Financial forecasts and revenue models

Clear, defensible models: revenue forecasts, pricing and ROI analysis, payback periods and break-even. Numbers you understand, can explain to an investor and can update yourself.

Market research

Customer, market and competitor research that answers the question you actually have, delivered as short, usable insights rather than a document that sits in a drawer.

Presentations and pitch decks

Investor decks, pitch decks and sales presentations with a sharp story, clean design and numbers that hold up. I have presented to rooms of 100+ including business angels, and I will help you do the same with confidence.
